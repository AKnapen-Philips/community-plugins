Enable the organization-team filter in the enterprise dashboard with
`copilot.enterpriseOrganizationTeams: true`. This option requires
`copilot.enterprise`, `copilot.organization`, and `copilot.ingestTeams` to be
configured. The enterprise view's Team filter is now labelled Enterprise team
to distinguish it from organization teams.
